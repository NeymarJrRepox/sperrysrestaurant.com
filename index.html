A Saratoga Tradition Since 1932

 

Where tradition Meets Inspiration

Sperry’s Restaurant offers a casual, yet elegant dining atmosphere, right in the heart of downtown Saratoga Springs, serving a rich combination of classic and contemporary American cuisine...with a bit of Global flare! Whether you are looking to enjoy a mouth-watering cut of steak or our wide array of fresh, local seafoods, you are sure to find the right dish on our menu to tantalize your senses.

Enjoy an award-winning wine list or one of our signature cocktails as you sit down in our historic Bar Room or any of our three other warm and inviting dining rooms—not to mention, the loveliest Garden Patio in Saratoga when the weather warms. No matter where you sit or what you choose to eat, we are sure you are going to love your experience at Sperry’s.
